Privacy Policy

Jupiter, built by Hie · Last updated 2 November 2026

In short: Jupiter is a private music locker. Your music is yours and stays private to your account. We do not sell your data, we show no ads, and we use no advertising or analytics trackers.

1. Who we are

Jupiter is a personal music app built by Hie (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what Jupiter collects, why, and the choices you have.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, display name and a password. Passwords are never stored in readable form; only a salted scrypt hash is kept.
  • Your music and library: audio files you upload, artwork you add, and the details you or your files provide (title, artist, album, genre, year), plus your playlists, favourites and settings.
  • Listening activity: which songs you play, skip or finish, and for how long. This powers your own stats, recently played and mixes in the app.
  • Session data: a sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 7 days, and basic technical data (such as IP address and request logs) handled by our hosting provider for security and reliability.

3. What stays on your device

Songs you choose to download for offline playback, and some app preferences, are stored on your device. Removing a download in the app, clearing the app’s storage, or uninstalling the app deletes them. The Android app also bundles its screens so it opens instantly; it needs an internet connection to load your library and stream music.

4. How we use it

  • To run Jupiter: sign you in, store and stream your music, build your playlists and stats.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • We do not use your music or listening data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it.

5. Where it is stored and who can see it

Your account data and music are stored with the storage and hosting providers used to run Jupiter (for example a cloud host and a private storage repository). Your uploads are private to your account and are not shared with other users. Our service providers process data only to host Jupiter for us. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

6. Your music and copyright

Only upload music you own or have permission to store. You are responsible for what you upload. We may remove content or suspend accounts that break the law or these rules.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete songs, playlists and downloads inside the app at any time, and export your library list from Settings. To delete your account and the data tied to it, contact us using the details below and we will remove it.

8. Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are hashed, and sign-in sessions expire. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

9. Children

Jupiter is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use of Jupiter after a change means you accept the updated policy.
